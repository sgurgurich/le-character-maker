Features

Everything a build test needs

Edits are queued and shown in plain words before anything is saved, and every save keeps a backup.

Item and unique creator

Any base with your choice of affixes, tiers and rolls, any unique or set item with Legendary Potential or Weaver's Will, and full legendaries. Items are written in the game's own format, byte for byte.

Build-ready in one click

Level 100, every passive point, all 20 idol slots, maxed skills, max faction rank and full crafting materials.

Import from LE Tools

Bring in a planner build: gear, idols, blessings, passives, skills and hotbar. Take everything, or pick the parts.

Skills and hotbar

Pick any of your class's skills for the five specialization slots, fully levelled, and arrange the hotbar.

Gear loadouts

Save a character's gear, idols and blessings, then copy them onto any character. Swapped-out gear is always kept.

Factions and favor

Join Circle of Fortune or the Merchant's Guild, max the rank, and set your favor.

Gold and materials

Gold, runes, glyphs, every affix shard, dungeon keys and Ancient Bones. The app shows which characters share the stash.

Safe by design

Saving is blocked while Last Epoch is running, every save backs up the previous version, and untouched data is written back exactly.

How it works

Three steps

No installer. The app keeps itself up to date and tells you when a new version is out.

Download and run

Grab LE-Character-Maker.exe and double-click it. It opens in its own window and finds your offline saves automatically.

Load item data (once)

For item names, the creator and build import, click Item data in the app and follow the three short steps using lastepochtools.com.

Make it build-ready

Create a character or pick one, click Make build-ready, add your gear, save, then launch Last Epoch and play offline.

FAQ

Questions

Does this work with online characters?

No. It only edits offline characters stored on your PC, in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Eleventh Hour Games\Last Epoch\Saves. Online characters live on Eleventh Hour Games' servers and can't be edited.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is the download safe?

The exe isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about new downloads. Click More info → Run anyway. The app is open source, each release is built by GitHub Actions from the public code, and every exe is published with a SHA-256 checksum.

Can it break my saves?

Every save is backed up first (open Backups in the app to restore one), saving is blocked while the game is running, and anything you didn't change is written back byte for byte. Still, test new edits on a throwaway character first.

Where does the item data come from?

Item, affix and unique names come from lastepochtools.com. The app doesn't redistribute that data: you export it from their site with a bookmark and load the file into the app.

How do updates work?

When you open the app, it checks GitHub for a newer release. If one exists, it shows what's new and offers Update now, which downloads the new exe, checks it against its SHA-256 checksum, swaps it in and restarts. Check for updates in the sidebar does the same on demand.
